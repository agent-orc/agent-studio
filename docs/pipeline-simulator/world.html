The world

One world shows the pipeline as places where cards wait and work. The owner first watches it move with real events, then forks it into a what-if run and plays with it. This page covers the two modes, the world, following one card, and the visual language taken from the Studio.

01 Two modes

Live draws the real pipeline of one project now. Its source is the event stream the Studio already has: lane events (IntegrationLaneEvent), step starts and ends in pipeline-execution.json, token receipts and quota snapshots, pushed over the existing /hubs/jobs hub. On opening, the view loads a snapshot of every card's phase, slot and waiting time and then applies events. It runs on the wall clock with no play controls, only a pause.

What-if forks the live state at a chosen moment, by default "now". Cards in flight keep their elapsed time, and the run goes forward with the current parameter set plus the owner's changes. The owner can change a strategy, a slot count, a duration or an arrival wave, then play, speed up, scrub, or run 200 seeds for a range. A what-if run can also start from a recorded day (replay). "Back to live" is always one click away.

The two modes are told apart by four signs at once, so that a simulated queue is never read as a real one:

  • the ground: plain paper in live, a fine blueprint grid in what-if;
  • the figures: solid in live, outlined in what-if;
  • the mode pill: "Live 14:05" with a steady dot, or "What-if from 14:05 · seed 7" with each owner change as a dashed chip next to it;
  • the timeline: one line up to now in live, and a fork marker followed by a dashed future in what-if.

02 The world

Each phase is a place on an isometric ground: a yard for Ready, a workshop with one desk per coding slot, a hall with one bench per review slot, the integration gate with its queue, and a yard for Done. Each card is a small figure. A blue figure is in its first pass. An orange figure is in rework: a sync round, a fix round, a repair, or a review after one of those. Return paths are drawn as dashed orange lines and carry a figure while a card is on them. The place that is the bottleneck right now gets the one highlight colour. One plain sentence above the world says what the picture shows, computed from the same numbers, for example "Integration is the bottleneck." A small bar at the top right shows today's list-price USD and quota points, split into first pass and rework.

The bottleneck is the place whose queue grows fastest over the last hour, weighted by how long its oldest card has waited. A full place whose queue does not grow is not the bottleneck, because it keeps up. In the mockup, Coding is full (10 of 10) but drains into Review as fast as cards arrive. The gate's queue grows hour after hour. When a what-if change moves the bottleneck, the highlight moves to the new place with a short transition, and the sentence above the world changes with it, for example "With fast as intended, coding becomes the bottleneck."

A place draws one figure per card up to 40, then a count. Under reduced motion figures do not walk; they change place between frames and every place shows its count.

03 Following one card

Clicking a figure, or choosing a card key, follows that card. The world dims the others, and a ribbon under the world shows each round as a sequence of segments: work in a slot, wait in a queue, and the reason for each return. Each round lists its model, tokens, list-price USD and the event that ended it. In live mode the ribbon is built from the card's records and links to the card (#/tasks/<key>). In a what-if run, a simulated card carries a "simulated" badge, and a recorded card shows its recorded past up to the fork and its simulated future after it.

04 Visual language

The inspiration's principle is kept: a light, airy world in thin lines, many small figures in two colours, one highlight, one plain statement, and pill controls. Its drawings, text, palette and fonts are not taken. Everything maps to the Studio's tokens and components, so the view works in both themes and needs no new colour system and no font from another host.
