01 Scope

An analysis of 17 and 18 November found that about 40 % of Claude usage was rework. 75 of 95 repeat coding runs since 15 November followed a review that had passed. In each case the delivery conflicted with develop at integration, the agent got a round to include develop, the new delivery went through the full review again, and by then develop had moved again. For Agent Studio the median batch gate latency is 133 minutes (p95 598), 48 % of batches are green, and every delivered card costs 1.9 full suite runs.

The owner decided the following between 20 and 22 November 2026. This Dossier takes those decisions as fixed and does not reopen them:

  • A merge conflict is an orchestration error, and specifically an error of the dispatcher. Selecting and ordering cards must prevent conflicts first. Cheaper conflict handling comes second.
  • Integrate first, then test, is the default. Only the automated tests move behind integration, and the model review aspects stay in front of it. This is configurable, and the fast mode is the default. For Agent Studio, develop and Stage get a light base suite and are then deployed. Only a Production release gets the full suite.
  • Efficiency comes before lead time.
  • The selection knows several strategies and applies them. Strategies belong to the persona and role model, and personas compete with each other.
  • The owner reviews personas by criteria that combine into one weighted value. The weights start with optimistic defaults, and the owner adjusts them afterwards.
  • A persona whose strategy is packaging may recut cards, merging overlapping findings into one larger card or into a chain. Every persona has its own rules.

This is a concept and changes no product code. It defines the role, five starter personas, the competition, the criteria and their weights, the two pages, and how cards are cut. It then splits the work into twelve slices that can be promoted to cards. Its main evidence is a replay of the 57 playthrough cards of 17 November. The replay was rebuilt from git, and its data and script are in replay/.

02 What exists

Most of the building blocks exist, but they are not connected. The most important finding has two parts. The local pick-gate already declares a scope from the paths named in a card's prompt and refuses to run two overlapping cards together. The remote claim path, which ran the 17 November wave, applies no scope rule at all. Neither path compares a candidate with cards that finished coding but have not integrated yet.

03 Role

Charter

Dispatcher. Decide which Ready card starts when, what runs alongside what, and what is chained, so that cards integrate without conflicts at the lowest token cost per accepted card while using the parallel slots the work allows. Explain every decision. Never change what a card asks for. Never move a card into Progress, Review, Human review or Done. Never lower a priority or move an owner pin. Never remove a dependency set by the owner, a Dossier or another persona. Never raise the slot count.

How a dispatcher runs

Dispatch decisions are frequent and cheap, so they must not be model sessions. A slot frees several times an hour, and a persona occurrence is a five-minute session that runs at most seven to ten times a week. A dispatcher persona is therefore a descriptor that names a strategy: a versioned rule set with parameters, evaluated by a pure policy in orchestration-decisions/, as the repository requires for orchestration rules. The host collects the facts, calls the policy, and applies the returned plan. The persona's sessions do the rest: the weekly report and, for packaging personas, the text of recut proposals.

Rights

Rights are capability-catalogue operations granted per persona (PT-D15, PT-D16). The tier follows the persona team rule. T1 is a bounded, reversible action under a standing grant. T2 is anything that bypasses a check, and it needs an explicit, time-boxed owner grant with a use cap (PT-D17). Shadow personas hold only observe and dispatch.shadow.record.

Reads granted by the role template: card metadata and prompts of Backlog and Ready (internal), forecasts, the conflict ledger, run and round ledgers, token use per card, slot state. Not granted: run logs, secrets, chat.

Reporting duty

  • Every decision writes one entry in the dispatcher log, which uses the fields of PersonaLogEntryV1 (PT-D18): decision id, strategy and revision, the rule that fired, the digest of the facts, the candidates it passed over and why, the expected overlap, and a templated reason of at most 280 characters. Shadow decisions write the same entry, marked shadow.
  • Every wave gets a model-free summary item in the persona feed once its last card integrates: cards, conflicts and their attribution, holds over 4 hours, chains, recuts, forecast accuracy, parallelism used.
  • Every week a report from the template dispatcher-v1, composed deterministically. One optional model call within the allowance writes the narrative.
  • Immediately: every conflict attributed to the live persona becomes a feed item with importance from a fact floor of 60, so it reaches the magazine.
